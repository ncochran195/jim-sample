# Log in

**Purpose:** logs in as the QA user.

**Inputs:** a Playwright `page`. The QA user's email and password come from `QA_USER_EMAIL` and `QA_USER_PASSWORD`.

**Outputs:** none; the page is on the projects page, its "Projects" heading showing.

**Preconditions:** no one is logged in.

**Flakiness:** none seen.
