# Open a project

**Purpose:** open a project's page from the "Projects" page by its name.

**Inputs:** `page`; `name`, the project's exact name.

**Outputs:** the project's path, such as `/projects/12`. The page ends on the project's page.

**Preconditions:** signed in; the project exists. When several projects share the name, the newest (top of the list) opens.

**Flakiness:** none.
